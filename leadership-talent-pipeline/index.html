What We're Looking For

The leaders we admire across our portfolio have the following traits:

  • Proactive with a bias towards action
  • Clear and responsive communicators
  • Love of learning and continuous growth
  • Unafraid to get hands dirty / do the work

If you are used to having large teams working under you and moving at a slow pace, our roles are probably not a good fit for you. We look for scrappy, fast learners who are focused on delivering value to our clients.
